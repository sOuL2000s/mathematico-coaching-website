About Dipanjan Chatterjee, M.Sc in Pure Maths

At MATHEMATICO, we believe that every student possesses an inherent ability to conquer the complexities of mathematics. Our philosophy centers on transforming daunting equations into engaging insights, building a robust foundation, and cultivating a profound understanding of mathematical principles.

Under the expert guidance of Dipanjan Chatterjee, M.Sc in Pure Maths, we employ innovative pedagogical approaches, provide meticulous personalized attention, and foster a nurturing learning ecosystem. Our focus is squarely on conceptual clarity, advanced problem-solving techniques, and rigorous examination readiness, empowering students to not just learn, but to truly excel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the batch timings and class frequency?

Our batches typically run twice a week for regular courses, with additional sessions for competitive exam preparation. Specific timings vary by batch and will be communicated upon enrollment. We offer both morning and evening slots to accommodate diverse schedules.

What is the fee structure for different courses?

Our fee structure is competitive and varies based on the course and its duration. For detailed fee information and any ongoing discounts, please contact us directly via phone or email, or visit our centers in Madhyamgram or Barasat. We're happy to discuss your specific needs.

Do you provide study materials and mock tests?

Absolutely. We provide comprehensive, meticulously crafted study materials, including detailed notes, extensive practice sheets, and curated recommended book lists. Regular mock tests are an integral part of our curriculum, conducted to assess student progress and thoroughly prepare them for examinations. Detailed performance analysis and personalized feedback are also provided to ensure continuous improvement.

What is the student-teacher ratio?

We are committed to providing personalized attention for every student. We maintain carefully controlled small batch sizes to foster an interactive, engaging learning environment where individual doubts can be addressed promptly and effectively, ensuring no student is left behind.
